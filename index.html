About

Sheet 01 / Overview

I'm a GIS professional and U.S. military veteran with a background spanning municipal government, environmental data systems, and geospatial instruction. My work centers on turning messy, disconnected data into systems people can actually rely on - clean parcel fabrics, cartographic standards that hold up at any scale, and automation that removes repetitive work from GIS teams.

I currently work as a GIS Project Manager and Analyst, and teach GIS as an adjunct instructor at the college level. I'm active in the geospatial professional community through MSGIC and URISA, and I hold an active Public Trust clearance.

I'm looking for my next role in a GIS or geospatial analysis position - government, private sector, or mission-focused - where precision and clear communication both matter.
